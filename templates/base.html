The Importance of Corn

Corn is one of the world's most important crops, serving as a staple food for millions and a key component in animal feed and industrial products. With global production exceeding 1 billion tons annually, maintaining healthy corn crops is crucial for food security and economic stability.

Water Requirements

Corn requires 20-35 inches of water during growing season for optimal yield.

Temperature

Ideal temperature range is 60-95°F with optimal growth at 75-86°F.

Sunlight

Corn needs full sun - at least 6-8 hours of direct sunlight daily.

Spacing

Plant seeds 8-12 inches apart in rows 30-36 inches apart.
